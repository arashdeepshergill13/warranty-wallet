Backed

Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Backed keeps your receipts and warranty details on your own device. We do not collect, receive or store any of your information. There are no accounts, no servers that hold your data, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you add is saved only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone:

  • receipt photos you scan or choose;
  • the details of each item: name, store, purchase date, price, warranty length, serial number and your notes;
  • your settings.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The app is built so that it cannot make connections to the internet.

How receipts are read

The text on a receipt is recognised on your device using text recognition built into iOS. Receipt photos are not uploaded and are not processed by any online or artificial-intelligence service.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan a receipt. Scans are saved only on your device.
  • Photos: only the photo you pick is given to the app.
  • Notifications: to remind you before a warranty ends. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS; no push service is involved.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: only if you turn on the app lock. The check is done by iOS. The app never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Each one is optional and can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, the app’s data is included in that backup, which is run by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple’s privacy policy. If you use “Back up everything” inside the app, the backup file goes only where you choose to save or send it. That file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Sharing

The app shares something only when you tap a share or export button, and only with the destination you pick in the iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete a single item from its page, or everything from Settings › Delete everything. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes all of its data. Because we never have a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

This page is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes standard request information such as IP address in order to deliver the page. The web version of the app available on this site works the same way as the iPhone app: what you add is stored only in your own browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever begins to collect any data, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
