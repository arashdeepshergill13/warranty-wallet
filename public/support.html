Backed

Help and contact

Backed keeps your receipts on your iPhone and reminds you before a warranty runs out.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question on the support board. Please don’t post receipt photos or personal details there.

Common questions

The app read my receipt wrong.
Receipts vary a lot. Every detail can be corrected on the review screen before you save, and later with the pencil button on the item. A flat, well-lit receipt scans best.

How does it know the warranty length?
It doesn’t. Unless your receipt states a warranty period, the app suggests a typical length for that kind of product and marks it as an estimate. Check the box, manual or manufacturer’s website and set the real length.

I’m not getting reminders.
Open iPhone Settings › Notifications › Backed and allow notifications. Then check Settings inside the app to see that reminders are on.

I got a new phone. How do I move my receipts?
Restoring your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings the app’s data with it. You can also use Settings › Back up everything on the old phone, send the file to the new phone, and use Restore from a backup.

I deleted the app. Can you recover my receipts?
No. Your data was only ever on your device, so there is no copy to recover unless you have a backup.

Is my data safe?
It never leaves your phone, iOS encrypts it at rest, and you can add a Face ID lock in Settings. See the privacy policy.
